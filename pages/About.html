Who We Are

ABOUT US

Empowering the Next Generation of Environmental Leaders

We are an Environmental Learning Centre, a dedicated learning facility promoting environmental awareness, education, and sustainable practices. The Centre targets schools, professionals, communities especially in fragile settings, and local organizations to address environmental challenges such as deforestation, climate change, water scarcity, and waste management.

We align with Zimbabwe's national environmental policies and the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs).
